Ricochet Pop: Bank Shot Puzzle

Privacy Policy and Support · a.k.soft games

Ricochet Pop: Bank Shot Puzzle is made for players of all ages, including children. It has no accounts, no purchases, no chat and no tracking. It shows only rewarded ads that the player chooses to watch, and every ad is child-directed (COPPA): contextual, never personalized.

This page covers Ricochet Pop: Bank Shot Puzzle (the "Game") for iPhone, published on the App Store by Andrii Kulyk as a.k.soft games ("we", "us"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. What we do not collect

We do not collect, send or share:

  • names, email addresses or any account information (there are no accounts)
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA): the Game never asks to track you
  • precise location, contacts, photos or files
  • usage statistics or crash reports of our own

Your progress (levels opened, stars) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your device. Deleting the Game deletes them.

Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics (such as downloads) from users who have agreed to share them with developers; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. Advertising

The Game shows only rewarded ads that the player chooses to watch: a hint that shows the best shot, or three more shots after running out. There are no banners and no ads between levels, and the first levels have no ad offers at all. Ads are non-personalized and contextual only: every player is treated as a child, and we do not use behavioral tracking or advertising identifiers.

To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, our advertising partners automatically process limited technical information: the IP address (used to estimate the country or region), device model, operating system and language, app version, non-advertising device or app identifiers, interactions with the ad, and diagnostic data. They are configured as child-directed under COPPA and may not use this information to build profiles or to personalize ads.

Our advertising partners:

  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) – Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads – Privacy Policy

3. Children and parents

The Game is directed to children as well as older players, and we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws: we do not request or collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13; we do not show personalized ads; there is no chat and nothing to buy. Links out of the Game (the other a.k.soft games on the App Store, this page) are in the settings and the MORE GAMES screen only.

Parents may ask us at any time to delete data held by the ad partners above: write to us and we will forward the request. Uninstalling the Game deletes the progress saved on the device.

4. Changes

When a future version changes what is processed, we update this page before it ships, and the date below.

5. Support and contact

Questions, ideas, a bug, or a level you could not beat? Write to kyluk5@gmail.com. We read everything.
